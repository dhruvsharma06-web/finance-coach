# Kosh: demo video script (target 3:30, ~470 words)

**Setup:** record at 1920×1080. Open https://finance-coach-six.vercel.app, then go to **Step inside Kosh → Explore the demo**. Press **Reset demo** in the sidebar before you start.
**Voice:** warm, unhurried Indian English. Say "rupees" and "lakh" naturally. Pause where you see `·`.
**On-screen numbers** come from the demo data. Don't change them, so the voiceover matches.

## Shot checklist
1. Landing hero → memory loop steps 1–5 (scroll slowly)
2. Import: the 6-month statement sample, then the result card
3. Transactions: the EatClub correction, the toast, the flashing rows (Undo is optional)
4. Dashboard: the headline and the money flow
5. Recurring: Netflix +30% and the "still using?" prompt
6. What if: the typed scenario, then Fix my plan
7. Family, Splits, Festivals
8. Ask Kosh (Hinglish), the Coach guardrail, Monthly review, Wrapped
9. Landing close

## Captions
Use the voiceover lines as captions, one row per `·` segment, at about 42 characters per line. Leave the Hinglish line as typed.

## Music and sound
Soft ambient piano or lo-fi at about −24 dB under the voice. Add a subtle "tick" when the rule is learned and a soft chime when Fix my plan lands. Only use royalty-free tracks (e.g. YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music), and credit them in the description.

## Honesty rules for the edit
Show only real app behaviour, with no invented users, testimonials or results. All numbers are the demo persona's. Kosh is educational and doesn't recommend products.
